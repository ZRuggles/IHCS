# Editing Your Website: Your Three Options

**Prepared for:** Innovation Healthcare Solutions
**Goal:** Let you log in and update your own website — program info, prices,
text, images, and class dates — without calling a developer every time.

This document walks through **three ways** to make that happen, what each one
costs, and the honest pros and cons of each. Read the summary, then the option
that interests you. No technical background needed.

> **Quick note on prices:** The monthly fees for the "ready-made" tools
> (Sanity and Contentful) are set by those companies and can change. The
> figures below are current as of early 2026 — please confirm the live price
> before committing. The developer (build) costs are fixed quotes.

## Option 1 — Sanity

**What it is:** A popular, ready-made content system. We connect it to your
website. You get a private login to a clean dashboard where you update your
programs, text, prices, dates, and images. When you hit "Publish," your live
website updates.

**How editing feels:** You go to your editing dashboard (a separate web
address from your public site). You see simple forms — e.g. a "Programs" list
where you click a program and change its price or description, or drag in a new
photo. It's organized and friendly, but it is *not* your actual website — it's
a control panel for it.

### Cost
- **Your monthly cost: $0.** Sanity's free plan includes up to 3 users, plenty
  of edits, and image hosting — comfortably more than a site your size needs.
- **If you ever outgrow the free plan:** the next tier is roughly **$15 per
  editor per month**. For a single-location school, you are very unlikely to
  ever need this.
- **One-time setup (paid to developer): under $500.** This covers moving your
  content in and wiring the dashboard to your site.

### What's included (free plan)
- Hosted login and dashboard (nothing for us to maintain)
- Image uploads with fast delivery (built in)
- Up to 3 user accounts
- Revision history (see and undo past changes)

### Upsides
- **Cheapest ongoing cost — likely free forever** for your use.
- Excellent, modern image handling.
- We maintain almost nothing — the company runs the dashboard.
- Your content can be **exported anytime** (you're never locked out of your own
  words and prices).

### Downsides
- You edit in a **separate dashboard**, not on your real website pages. Some
  owners find that less intuitive at first.
- You depend on an **outside company** continuing to offer a fair free plan.
- If you ever leave Sanity, the website has to be re-wired by a developer
  (your content comes with you, but the connection does not).

## Option 2 — Contentful

**What it is:** Very similar to Sanity — another well-known, ready-made content
system with a hosted editing dashboard. We connect it to your site the same way.

**How editing feels:** Nearly identical to Sanity — a separate, form-based
dashboard where you update programs, text, and images, then publish.

### Cost
- **Your monthly cost: $0.** Contentful's free ("Free Space") plan covers a
  small business site with room to spare.
- **If you outgrow it:** Contentful's paid plans jump up significantly (their
  next standard tier has historically been **$300+/month**), which is far more
  than a school site needs — but it's worth knowing the paid step is steep.
- **One-time setup (paid to developer): under $500.**

### What's included (free plan)
- Hosted login and dashboard
- Image uploads and delivery (built in)
- A small team of users
- Revision history

### Upsides
- **Free** for your size.
- Mature, widely used, reliable.
- Good image handling.
- Content is exportable.

### Downsides
- Same as Sanity: you edit in a **separate dashboard**, not on the live page.
- Depends on an **outside company**.
- The **jump from free to paid is large** if you ever cross the free limits —
  bigger cliff than Sanity. (For your size, you should stay on free, but it's
  an honest risk to know about.)

## Option 3 — We Build It Into Your Site (Custom)

**What it is:** Instead of renting an outside dashboard, we build the editing
ability **directly into your own website**. You go to a private login page,
sign in, and then **browse your real site and click edit icons** right on the
page to change text, prices, images, and class dates. Your changes save to a
database that we set up, and your site loads from it.

**How editing feels:** This is the experience most people picture — you're
looking at your actual homepage or program page, you click a little pencil next
to the text or image, you change it, and it's saved. No separate dashboard to
learn; you edit what you see.

### Cost
- **Your monthly cost: $7/month.** This is the hosting fee for the database and
  the small server that powers the login and saving. (We deliberately use the
  affordable tier — there's a $25/month "pro" tier we do **not** need for your
  site.)
- **One-time setup (paid to developer): $500 or more.** This is more than the
  ready-made options because we're building the login, the database, the
  editing icons, image uploads, and security from scratch, specifically for you.

### What's included
- A private login page just for you (and any staff you choose)
- Click-to-edit on your real website pages
- Full add/edit/delete for programs (create a new course, change a price,
  remove an old class)
- Editable general text (headlines, contact info, hours)
- Editable class schedules and dates
- Image uploads
- Everything owned by you — no outside company involved

### Upsides
- **The editing experience you actually want:** edit right on your live pages.
- **You own 100% of it** — no third-party company, no risk of someone else
  changing their prices or rules.
- Fully customizable — if you want a new kind of edit later, we can add it.
- No dependence on an outside service's free plan staying free.
- **It's future-proof** — it sets the foundation for bigger features down the
  road (see the box below).

> ### Why this one grows with your business
> The ready-made tools (Sanity and Contentful) are excellent at **one job** —
> editing the words and pictures on your site. They are **not built to run an
> actual application.** So if later you want things like:
> - **Student accounts** (each student logs in to their own page)
> - **Payment tracking** (see who's paid in full, who's on a plan, who still owes)
> - **A smarter application page** that saves applicants into a list
> - **A simple mini-CRM** to manage applicants and students
>
> …a CMS can't do those on its own. You'd have to add a **separate system
> alongside it** — meaning two tools, two bills, and two things to keep in sync.
>
> The custom build avoids that. Because it already includes a **login and a
> database**, it's the very same foundation those bigger features get built on.
> We do the editing piece now, and if you ever want to expand, we **add to what's
> already there** instead of starting over or running a second system.
>
> **Bottom line:** this option costs a little more today, but it's the one that
> grows *with* your business instead of boxing you in later.

### Downsides
- **Small monthly cost ($7)** where the ready-made tools are free.
- **Higher one-time build cost** ($500+ vs. under $500).
- **We maintain it** — security, updates, and backups are our responsibility
  rather than a big company's. (This is fine, but it's a real difference: a
  login that can change your live site has to be kept secure.)
- Takes a bit longer to build than connecting a ready-made tool.

## How to Choose — Plain-English Guide

**Choose Sanity if:** you want the **lowest possible cost** (free), don't mind
editing in a separate, tidy dashboard, and are comfortable relying on a
well-known outside company. *This is the cheapest, lowest-maintenance path.*

**Choose Contentful if:** you specifically prefer Contentful — but for your
size it's essentially the same as Sanity with a steeper "if you ever outgrow
it" price. *Sanity is usually the better pick of the two.*

**Choose the Custom Build if:** you want to **click and edit right on your real
website**, you want to **fully own** the system with no outside company, you
think you might want **bigger features later** (student accounts, payment
tracking, a mini-CRM), and the **$7/month + higher one-time cost** is worth that
to you. *This is the only option that can grow into a full application later
without adding a second system.*

### A Note on "Free"

Sanity and Contentful are free *for sites your size*. That's genuine — but
"free" means you're using their generous starter plan. If your needs ever grow
a lot, those plans have paid tiers (Sanity's is gentle; Contentful's jumps
higher). The custom build avoids that question entirely: a flat $7/month, no
surprises, no outside pricing decisions.
