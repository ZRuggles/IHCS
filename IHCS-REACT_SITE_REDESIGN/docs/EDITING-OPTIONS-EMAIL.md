------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 1 — SANITY
------------------------------------------------------------

What it is: A ready-made editing system I connect to your site. You get a
private login to a clean dashboard where you update programs, prices, text,
dates, and photos. Hit "Publish" and your live site updates.

How it feels: You go to a separate editing page (different web address from
your public site) and fill in simple forms — click a program, change its price,
drag in a new photo. Friendly and organized, but it's a control panel for your
site, not your actual site.

Your cost: $0/month (their free plan fits you with room to spare). Setup paid
to me: under $500.

The good:
  • Cheapest ongoing cost — likely free forever for your size
  • Great photo handling, built in
  • I maintain almost nothing; the company runs it
  • Your content can be exported anytime — you're never locked out of it

The trade-offs:
  • You edit in a separate dashboard, not on your real pages
  • You're relying on an outside company keeping a fair free plan
  • If you ever left Sanity, the site would need to be re-connected by a developer

------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 2 — CONTENTFUL
------------------------------------------------------------

What it is: Almost the same as Sanity — another well-known ready-made system
with a hosted editing dashboard.

How it feels: Nearly identical to Sanity — a separate dashboard where you edit
and publish.

Your cost: $0/month (free plan covers your size). Setup paid to me: under $500.

The good:
  • Free for your size
  • Mature, reliable, widely used
  • Good photo handling
  • Content is exportable

The trade-offs:
  • Same as Sanity — you edit in a separate dashboard, not on the live page
  • Relies on an outside company
  • If you ever outgrew the free plan, their next step up is expensive (much
    more than Sanity's). You'd stay on free, but it's worth knowing.

(Honestly, for your size Sanity and Contentful are very similar, and I'd lean
Sanity of the two.)

------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION 3 — CUSTOM BUILD (I BUILD IT INTO YOUR SITE)
------------------------------------------------------------

What it is: Instead of renting an outside dashboard, I build the editing right
into your own website. You go to a private login, sign in, then browse your
real site and click little edit icons on the page to change text, prices,
photos, and class dates. Your changes save to a database I set up, and your
site loads from it.

How it feels: This is the experience most people picture — you're looking at
your actual homepage or a program page, you click a pencil next to the text or
photo, change it, and it's saved. Nothing separate to learn; you edit what you
see.

Your cost: $7/month (hosting for the database and login — I'm using the
affordable tier on purpose; there's a pricier option we don't need). Setup paid
to me: $500 or more (it's more because I'm building the login, database,
edit icons, photo uploads, and security specifically for you).

The good:
  • The editing experience you actually want — edit right on your live pages
  • You own 100% of it — no outside company, no one else's pricing or rules
  • Fully customizable; we can add new kinds of edits later
  • A flat $7/month with no surprises
  • It sets the foundation for the future (see below)

Why this one is "future-proof":
The ready-made tools (Sanity and Contentful) are great at ONE thing — editing
the words and pictures on your site. They are not built to run an actual
application. So if down the road you want things like student accounts, a way
to track who's paid and who still owes, a smarter application page, or even a
simple mini-CRM to manage your applicants and students — a CMS can't do that on
its own. You'd have to add a separate system alongside it, meaning two tools,
two bills, and two things to keep in sync.

The custom build avoids that entirely. Because it already includes a login and
a database, it's the same foundation those bigger features would be built on.
We do the editing piece now, and if you ever want to expand later, we add to
what's already there instead of starting over or bolting on a second system.
In short: this option costs a little more today, but it's the one that grows
WITH your business instead of boxing you in.

The trade-offs:
  • A small monthly cost where the ready-made tools are free
  • Higher one-time build cost
  • I maintain it (security, updates, backups) rather than a big company — that's
    fine, but it's a real difference worth naming
  • Takes a little longer to build than connecting a ready-made tool
